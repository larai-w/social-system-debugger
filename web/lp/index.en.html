What this material holds itself to

Handling how societies break carries a responsibility for how it is written.

  • No real place names, people, or live politics. Neither as criticism nor as praise. It speaks in abstracted types.
  • Not about any particular society. It is designed as structure that any society can meet.
  • It collects nothing. No sign-up, and what you do stays on your device.
  • The formulas and thresholds are public. You can check the arithmetic, not just the conclusion.
